In short

This site has no analytics, sets no cookies and sends nothing you type to any server.

This site

Pages, fonts and images are served from the same place as the page itself. No third-party scripts, trackers or embeds are loaded. Your browser remembers, for the current tab only, that you have seen the opening animation, so it does not play twice; that note is not sent anywhere and disappears when the tab closes.

The membership form

The “Become a member” form checks what you typed inside your browser and then prepares an email to hello@ffdev.studio in your own email app. Nothing is transmitted until you choose to send that email yourself, and you can read and change it first.

If you email us

When you send that email, or contact us any other way, FF Dev Studio holds what you sent as correspondence — your name, contact details and what you tell us about your site — to reply, recommend a plan, prepare a proposal, do the work you ask for and keep the records the law requires. We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising.

Your rights

Under Malaysia’s Personal Data Protection Act 2010 you can ask to see, correct or withdraw consent for the personal data we hold about you. Write to hello@ffdev.studio.
